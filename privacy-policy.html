Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Your words and progress stay on your device

Lexi stores the words you add and your study history (which cards you reviewed and how you rated them) only on your iPhone or iPad. We do not run servers, and we never receive, read or sell your words or progress. Spoken pronunciations are generated on your device by Apple's speech synthesizer.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, Lexi asks for notification permission and schedules a local reminder on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

Advertising

Lexi is free thanks to ads from Google AdMob (banner, interstitial and rewarded ads). AdMob may collect device identifiers, approximate location and ad interaction data to show and measure ads, as described in Google's advertising policy. Where required, you're asked for consent first, and Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt lets you decline tracking. Your words and progress are never shared with advertisers.

Backups and deletion

Your data is included in your device's own iCloud or computer backups if you have those turned on. You can delete your own words, or reset all progress, from the app. Deleting the app removes all its data from the device.

Children

Lexi is not directed at children under 13.
